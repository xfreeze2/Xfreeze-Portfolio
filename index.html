The ideas behind my work.

I am curious about where technology is taking us and what that future could mean for everyday people. I share my honest perspectives on artificial intelligence, engineering, space exploration and the people building ambitious technologies. My goal is to understand complex ideas and explain them in clear language that anyone can follow.

01

Learning in public.

I also write about everyday knowledge, overlooked ideas and assumptions that deserve a closer look. I do not claim to have every answer. I am constantly learning, questioning what I know and sharing what I discover along the way. For me, meaningful conversations begin with curiosity, honesty and a genuine desire to understand the world better.

02

Supporting the people who build.

A large part of my work is dedicated to understanding and supporting builders. Meaningful progress rarely happens without enormous personal sacrifice, years of uncertainty and the courage to pursue ideas that may initially seem impossible. These builders are helping shape the future of civilization, and I want more people to understand the importance of their work and where it could lead us.

03

A future inspired by Elon.

Elon Musk has significantly influenced how I think about progress and the future. What inspires me is not only the scale of the problems he pursues, but the persistence required to keep working when success is uncertain. His work across artificial intelligence, robotics, sustainable energy, global connectivity and space exploration has shown me that the future does not have to be a slightly improved version of the present. Technology can expand human capability, make knowledge more accessible and remove limitations that once seemed permanent.

That is why the idea of abundance matters so much to me. A world where intelligence is widely available, physical labour is no longer scarce, energy is cleaner and humanity can reach beyond Earth could give people greater freedom to learn, create and explore. That future must be built by people willing to accept uncertainty and pursue ideas others consider impossible. Their example motivates me to keep learning, explain these developments clearly and create work that helps more people understand the possibilities ahead.

I want to contribute to that change in my own way: by learning openly, communicating clearly, supporting those building the future and creating useful work that helps more people understand and participate in what comes next.
